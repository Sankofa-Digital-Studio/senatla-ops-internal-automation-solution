Historical costing and asset review

Source-backed decisions from the owner-supplied archive · 6 November 2026

Decision · Costing · Assets · Acceptance

Decision

  1. Evidence: The archive contains demolition, asbestos, transport, labour, equipment and asset records. The village grand-total formula contains #REF!; the asbestos workbook has conflicting P&G assumptions; an older dam quote uses 14% VAT.
  2. Challenge: Historical prices depend on the project and date. Asset rows lack verified expiry dates and some identifiers are missing or duplicated.
  3. Resolution: Show archived values as references. A user must review and add a cost line before it changes the estimate. Asset records enter only through the existing controlled preview after manual checks.

Before issuing a proposal: Confirm the signed contract party, scope, current rates, licensed asbestos handling and tax terms. Preset client names are provisional.

Costing workflow

Choose a scenario in the Director costing model. Open Historical References, inspect the cited workbook cell, and check the current supplier price. Copy a reference to Additional Costs, correct quantity, unit, rate and source, then add it. The waterfall, cashflow and CSV update. Removing a line reverses it.

Asset review workflow

Run python scripts/review-historical-asset-registers.py <archive.zip>. The report gives aggregate counts and row numbers, without names, plates or serial values. Securely inspect flagged rows, verify ownership, current site and compliance dates, then use the existing asset CSV preview. Never invent an expiry date. Historical fuel-list employees are not current custodians. Candidate screening sheets are excluded.

Acceptance and release boundary

  • Default estimates stay unchanged until a line is added.
  • Additional spend is separated in the waterfall and CSV.
  • Invalid quantities and rates are rejected; exported text is spreadsheet-safe.
  • Asset exceptions require manual review before import.
  • The client warning appears on screen and in CSV.

These archive findings do not certify a tender or change database access. Production promotion remains a separate release step.
