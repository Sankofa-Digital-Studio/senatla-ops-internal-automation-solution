Formats identified

The archive contains village-by-village quotations, asbestos schedules of rates, sectioned civil bills of quantities, kilometre schedules and detailed labour, equipment and materials breakdowns. West Gold and Tau Mine use existing workspace phase structures as baseline formats. Quantities, prices and commercial percentages are excluded from the new templates.

The village quote contains a broken grand-total formula and the asbestos workbook has conflicting P&G assumptions. These source issues reinforce the decision to use layout only.
