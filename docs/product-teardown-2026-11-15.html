Senatla Ops Product Teardown

Verdict: Senatla Ops has a credible operational core. Its strongest idea is an evidence-backed operating heartbeat connecting field activity, office controls, and director decisions. Protect that core from demo residue, workflow sprawl, and unproven adoption assumptions.

Devil’s advocate

Platform before tool

The surface is broad. A new user may understand the architecture but not the first five-minute task. Do not add modules; make each role home answer what is incomplete, urgent, and safe to finish.

Demo data and trust

Hosted mode correctly refuses local records, but demo-shaped identifiers and mock recovery paths remain in local services. Keep fixtures behind an unmistakable local-review boundary.

Auditability versus discoverability

Recorded proof that is hidden behind a tab does not help a pressured approver. Bring evidence status and provenance into approval, export, sync, and asset commitment moments.

Offline proof

Use plain-language states: Saved on this device, Waiting to sync, Synced to Senatla Ops, or Needs attention.

Polished this pass

  • Made public sign-in explicit beside the arrow icon.
  • Replaced the faux clickable scroll control with a native keyboard-accessible button.
  • Updated the landing component test to protect both changes.

Priority sequence

  1. Make role home screens action-oriented.
  2. Standardise evidence summaries across commitment flows.
  3. Make sync states explicit and recoverable.
  4. Add hosted-build checks for local-only affordances.
  5. Validate three deterministic role journeys before expanding scope.
Bottom line: the product’s heart is the trustworthy chain from work performed to evidence captured to decision made.
